## Intro

So you are ready to create your first Product Recommendation Quiz.

The most successful quizzes have two things in common. They are **short** and they are **informative**. You do not need a long questionnaire to turn a browser into a customer.

**Objective**: Say you run a cosmetics shop with a selection of cleansers for different skin types. This tutorial builds a short quiz that helps a customer choose the best cleanser.

## Add a new quiz

=== "Shopify"

    1. **Add a new quiz from your [dashboard](/reference/dashboard/) by clicking `Create new quiz`.**
    2. **Open the [Quiz builder](/reference/quiz-builder/).** This is where you create your quiz.
    3. **Click on the `+ Add Question` button and select the question type you want to add.**
    4. **Edit the question and see how it looks in the preview.**
    5. **To change [question settings](/reference/quiz-builder/questions/#question-settings), click on the question card and use the settings panel on the right.** Here, you can change things like adding a question description or an image.
    6. **To make the quiz more personal, add a `Name question`.** You can then recall the answer elsewhere in the quiz with a [content dynamic source](/how-to-guides/use-information-recalls/).
    7. **Add a question for each thing the recommendation needs.** To select the best cleanser you need the customer's age, skin type and environment. A `Multiple-choice question` is usually the best way to collect all three. Start by asking about skin type.
    8. **Finally, find out the environment they live in.** Use a `Picture question` here, to make the quiz more interesting.
    9. **Remember to add an `email question`.** That is how you collect leads for your marketing campaigns. In the question settings you can make this one optional.
    10. **Once you have all your questions you can move them around by drag and drop.**
    11. **Keep the quiz short.** Customers typically drop out after question 8, and almost nobody reaches question 12. There is power in simplicity.

## Changing design

Now style the quiz.

=== "Shopify"

    1. **Open the [Quiz design](/reference/quiz-builder/quiz-design/) tab.**
    2. **Pick a theme under [`Color scheme`](/reference/quiz-builder/quiz-design/#my-themes).** It holds a set of ready-made themes.
    3. **Or you can create your own design by clicking on different elements in the editing panel.**
    4. **Match the quiz to your store's style:**
        - edit the font,
        - change the background color,
        - remove or add a background image,
        - and change the color of the buttons and choices.
    5. **[Add custom CSS](/how-to-guides/customize-quiz-design/#advanced-customizations-css)** if you want the quiz to look like nothing else.
    6. **Leave the design when you are happy with it.** Your changes save automatically.

## Linking products

Once you are happy with the design, add products to each question. This step matters.

=== "Shopify"

    1. **Check the dashboard.** It tells you when answers have no products linked to them.
    2. **To add a product, open the [choice settings](/reference/quiz-builder/questions/#choice-settings) and click on the [Upvote](/reference/quiz-builder/link-products/) section.**
    3. **Pick products from the list**, by scrolling or by typing a name. The list holds your synced store products.
    4. **Assign as many products to one answer as you need.**
    5. **The quiz works like an [upvoting system](/how-to-guides/recommend-products/).**
        - Products are linked to each choice.
        - When a customer picks that choice, all linked products receive one upvote.
        - After the customer takes the quiz, the results page will show the products sorted by the number of upvotes.
        - If there is a draw in the number of upvotes, the order depends on your Catalog mode setting. By default, ties are randomized. Turn on `Preserve collection order` in [`App settings > Catalog`](/reference/app-settings/#catalog) to show products in the same order as your Shopify collections.
    6. **Link products to every question and choice in the quiz.**
    7. **Click `Save`, then `Preview`** to test different answering routes and their results.


=== "Shopify (Legacy)"

    1. **Check the [success checklist](/reference/dashboard/#success-checklist).** It tells you when answers have no products linked to them.
    2. **To add a product, go to the [Link Products](/reference/quiz-builder/link-products/) tab and click on the blank space after the answer.**
    3. **Pick products from the list**, by scrolling or by typing a name. The list holds your synced store products.
    4. **Assign as many products to one answer as you need.**
    5. **The quiz works like an [upvoting system](/how-to-guides/recommend-products/).**

        - If there is a draw in the number of upvotes, the app will randomize the products.
    6. **Link products to every question and choice in the quiz.**
    7. **Click `Publish` to update the preview and the live quiz.** Then click `Preview` to test different answering routes and results.

## Editing the results page

Speaking of results. Make sure to also edit the Results Page.

=== "Shopify"

    1. **As in the Quiz builder, you can add multiple sections to your [Results page](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/) by clicking on the `Results Page` tab.**
    2. **Click on the `+ Add Block` button to select a block type.**
        - In `text blocks`, you can write insightful information about the product recommended.
        - With an `image block`, you can add your brand's logo.
        - In the `button block`, you can add a button to a custom URL.
        - In the `Products block`, you can select how many products will be recommended.


=== "Shopify (Legacy)"

    1. **Add sections to your [Results Page](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/)**, the same way you do in the Quiz Builder.
    2. **Click on a `+` plus sign to select a block type.**
        - In `content blocks`, you can write insightful information about the product recommended.
        - With an `image block`, you can add your brand's logo.
        - In the `Products block`, you can select how many products will be recommended.
    3. **You can use the [Markdown language](/how-to-guides/use-markdown/) to make your quiz pop.** Markdown lets you add **bold** or *emphasis* to your text. You can also insert links, images and videos. For more about Markdown, see [how to use markdown language](/how-to-guides/use-markdown/).
    4. **The [Results Page settings](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/) let you add a background image to the quiz.**

=== "WooCommerce"

    1. **Add sections to your [Results Page](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/)**, the same way you do in the Quiz Builder.
    2. **Click on a `+` plus sign to select a block type.**
        - In `content blocks`, you can write insightful information about the product recommended.
        - With an `image block`, you can add your brand's logo.

## Publishing the quiz

=== "Shopify"

    1. **Once your quiz is set up, go to your [Dashboard](/reference/dashboard/) and click `Make default`.**
    2. **Go to the [`Publish`](/reference/quiz-builder/share-publish/) section to see all the publishing options.**
    3. **For example, you can [embed a link to the quiz in your website's menu](/how-to-guides/publish-quiz-link/#link-popup-in-website-menu).**
    4. **In Shopify, go to `Online Store > Themes` and click `Customize`.**
    5. **Under `App embeds`, find `Link Popup Quiz` and activate it.**
    6. **In the settings, adjust the quiz width and height.** Then click `Save`.
    7. **In Shopify, go to `Content > Menus`, select the main menu and click `+ Add menu item`.**
    8. **In the `Title` field, enter a title such as `Take our Quiz`.** Under the `link` section, type `#quiz` and click the link to accept it.
    9. **Save the menu.**
    10. **Test the quiz on your live site** by clicking the link in the menu.

    You are done.

    3. **Adjust the quiz's width and height and generate a code.**
    4. **In your website's menu, you can add a new section and paste the code.**
