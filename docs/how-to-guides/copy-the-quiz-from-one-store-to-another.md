# How to Copy the Quiz from One Store to Another

A quiz can be exported from one store and imported into another. The design, the questions, the tags and the results page all come across.

Products and collections do not. The new store gives them different IDs, so you link them to the choices again once the quiz arrives.

## Copying the quiz

=== "Shopify"

    <div style="position: relative; padding-bottom: 56.34837355718783%; height: 0;"><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/KQlWuEbHHXQ?si=uMw8HqQ156H8SMGb" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%;"></iframe></div>

    !!! warning "Install the app on both stores first"

        The quiz can only arrive somewhere the app is already running. See [How to Install the App](/how-to-guides/install-app/).

    !!! info "Moving a quiz between versions is a different job"

        To bring a quiz from the legacy app into the Built for Shopify version on the same store, use [Migrate from Legacy App](/how-to-guides/migrate-shopify-legacy-quiz/) instead.

    1. **Go to [Quiz settings > General](/reference/quiz-builder/quiz-settings/) in the store you are copying from.**

    2. **Find `Export quiz to another store` and click `Copy quiz code`.** This produces a piece of JSON holding the whole quiz. It does not expire, but it captures the quiz as it is at that moment.

        ![Export Quiz Step](/images/manual_shopifyV2_quizbuilder_quizsettings.png)

        !!! tip "Keep a copy of the code"

            Paste it into a text file, so it cannot be lost.

    3. **Sign in to the other store in a private or incognito window.** Being signed in to two accounts in one browser causes caching issues.

    4. **Open the RevenueHunt app from that store's dashboard.**

    5. **On the [Dashboard](/reference/dashboard/), click [New Quiz](/reference/dashboard/#new-quiz).**

        ![manual_shopifyV2_createquiz](/images/manual_shopifyV2_createquiz.png)

    6. **Choose `Import quiz` and paste the code in.**

        ![Import Quiz Step](/images/manual_shopifyV2_createquiz_importquiz.png)

    The quiz appears on your dashboard, named with an `- imported from QUIZID` suffix.

    5. **On the [Dashboard](/reference/dashboard/), click [New Quiz](/reference/dashboard/#new-quiz).**

        ![Import Quiz Step](/images/how_to_copy_the_quiz_step_3.gif)

    The quiz appears on your dashboard, named with a `(copy)` suffix.

## Changing domains

Read this before you move the app to a different domain.

=== "Shopify"

    Changing only the domain name, from `example.com` to `example-two.com`, leaves the app and your quizzes alone. The `.myshopify.com` address stays the same, so nothing breaks.

    If the `.myshopify` account itself changes, that is a new store. Follow [Copying the quiz](#copying-the-quiz), then link your products to the choices again, because the new store gives them different product IDs.

    !!! warning "Use a private window for the second account"

        Signing in to two Shopify accounts in one browser causes caching issues.

=== "Shopify (Legacy)"

    Changing only the domain name, from `example.com` to `example-two.com`, leaves the app and your quizzes alone. The `.myshopify.com` address stays the same, so nothing breaks.

    If the `.myshopify` account itself changes, that is a new store. Follow [Copying the quiz](#copying-the-quiz), then link your products to the choices again, because the new store gives them different product IDs.

    !!! warning "Use a private window for the second account"

        Signing in to two Shopify accounts in one browser causes caching issues.

=== "WooCommerce"

    Changing only the domain name, from `example.com` to `example-two.com`, leaves the app and your quizzes alone. The WordPress account stays the same, so nothing breaks.

    When the WordPress account itself changes, copy the quiz across first, as [Copying the quiz](#copying-the-quiz) explains. Then link your products to the choices again, because the new store gives them different product IDs.

    !!! warning "Use a private window for the second account"

        Signing in to two WordPress accounts in one browser causes caching issues.

=== "Magento"

    Changing only the domain name, from `example.com` to `example-two.com`, leaves the app and your quizzes alone. The Magento account stays the same, so nothing breaks.

    When the Magento account itself changes, copy the quiz across first, as [Copying the quiz](#copying-the-quiz) explains. Then link your products to the choices again, because the new store gives them different product IDs.

    !!! warning "Use a private window for the second account"

        Signing in to two Magento accounts in one browser causes caching issues.

=== "BigCommerce"

    Changing only the domain name, from `example.com` to `example-two.com`, leaves the app and your quizzes alone. The BigCommerce account stays the same, so nothing breaks.

    When the BigCommerce account itself changes, copy the quiz across first, as [Copying the quiz](#copying-the-quiz) explains. Then link your products to the choices again, because the new store gives them different product IDs.

    !!! warning "Use a private window for the second account"

        Signing in to two BigCommerce accounts in one browser causes caching issues.

=== "Standalone"

    This version does not depend on your domain, and one quiz can run on several domains at once. Changing a domain breaks nothing.

    Update the product links in the [Catalogue](https://admin.revenuehunt.com/catalogue), or in your Google Product Feed, so they point at the new address.

!!! tip "Unsure about a move"

    [Contact support](/how-to-guides/contact-customer-support/) before you change anything.
