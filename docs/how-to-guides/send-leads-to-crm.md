# How to Send Quiz Leads to Your CRM

Connect your quiz to a CRM service such as Klaviyo or Mailchimp, and every contact from the quiz is added to your account there. You can then build email campaigns around what each customer answered.

## Direct integrations

### [:fontawesome-brands-shopify: Shopify customers](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-shopify-customers/)

Every customer who leaves an email in the quiz is added to your Shopify Customers list. Their profile carries the name, email, phone number and [customer tags](/reference/quiz-builder/customer-tags/) from the quiz.

[:fontawesome-solid-arrow-right: send leads to Shopify Customers](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-shopify-customers/) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [:fontawesome-solid-arrow-right: automate with Shopify Flow](/how-to-guides/automate-quiz-completions-with-shopify-flow/)

### [:material-wifi: Klaviyo](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-klaviyo/)

Every contact from the quiz is added to your Klaviyo Profiles, and can be added to a List at the same time. Segment those profiles on the quiz answers, then build an email flow for each segment.

[:fontawesome-solid-arrow-right: learn more](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-klaviyo/) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [:fontawesome-solid-arrow-right: build post-quiz lifecycle flows](/how-to-guides/send-klaviyo-post-quiz-email-flows/)

### [:material-hubspot: HubSpot](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-hubspot/)

Quiz results are sent to your HubSpot account, so you can segment your contacts on their answers and write a campaign for each group.

[:fontawesome-solid-arrow-right: learn more](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-hubspot/)

### [:fontawesome-brands-mailchimp: Mailchimp](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-mailchimp/)

Quiz data is sent to your Mailchimp account, so you can segment your audience and follow up by [customer tags](/reference/quiz-builder/customer-tags/). The Mailchimp integration sends only the email, the name and the customer tags. If you need to send more than that, such as the recommended products, pick a different service.

[:fontawesome-solid-arrow-right: learn more](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-mailchimp/)

### [:material-information-slab-circle: Omnisend](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-omnisend/)

Quiz results are sent to your Omnisend mailing list as each customer finishes. Segment that list on the answers, then follow up with a campaign for each segment.

[:fontawesome-solid-arrow-right: learn more](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-omnisend/)

### [:material-asterisk: Zapier](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-zapier/)

Zapier connects to thousands of apps, including most CRMs. Send your quiz leads to Zapier, and Zapier passes them on to any service it supports.

[:fontawesome-solid-arrow-right: learn more](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-zapier/)

### [:material-greater-than-or-equal: ActiveCampaign](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-activecampaign/)

Every quiz response becomes an ActiveCampaign contact, with the answers attached as custom fields and the quiz tags as native tags.

[:fontawesome-solid-arrow-right: learn more](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-activecampaign/)

### [:material-webhook: Webhooks](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-webhooks/)

A webhook sends the full quiz data to any endpoint you choose: the answers, the recommended products and the customer's contact details.

[:fontawesome-solid-arrow-right: learn more](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-webhooks/)

## Indirect integrations

If your CRM service is not in the list above, route the data through [Zapier](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-zapier/) or [Webhooks](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-webhooks/) instead. Both can forward it to a service the app does not support directly.

You can also [send result emails from the app](/how-to-guides/send-result-emails/), or [download all responses as a CSV file](/how-to-guides/download-quiz-responses/) and upload that file to the other service yourself.
