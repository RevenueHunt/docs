    - **Live chat** is for merchants on a paid plan, inside the app.
    - **Video calls** are for merchants on Pro and above.

### What support can help you with

- Using the Quiz Builder
- Customizing the quiz design with our built-in tools
- Understanding the recommendation algorithm
- Integrating the quiz with other apps
- Interpreting quiz metrics and analytics
- Troubleshooting why a product is or is not recommended in the results

If your question needs more than an email exchange, we offer a live one-to-one call.

### What support cannot do

- Add custom behavior with JavaScript
- Add custom styles with CSS
- Debug your store setup or configuration
- Debug your theme
- Create or edit Klaviyo templates

The app lets you add custom JavaScript and CSS to your quiz. We are a small team, and we cannot write or maintain that code for you.

Without a developer of your own, hire a freelancer on [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/) or [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/). For a Klaviyo template, look for a [Klaviyo template developer](https://www.fiverr.com/gigs/klaviyo-template).

## Can you build a quiz for me?

No. Our support team explains how the app works, and points you to the right articles and videos. We do not build quizzes.

To have one built and styled for you, hire a freelancer on [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/) or [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/). Any front-end developer can do it. Search those sites for `quiz` to find someone who has built one before.
