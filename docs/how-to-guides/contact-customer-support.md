!!! info "Contact Hours"

    **Availability**: Our support team is available Monday through Friday, from 7am to 11pm (GMT).

    **Response Time**: Expect a reply within 2-3 hours during business hours.

## Check app status

Check our [app status page](https://status.revenuehunt.com/) first, to see whether a known issue is affecting the app.


## What level of support is offered

We offer email support on every plan, free and paid.

Live chat is for merchants on a paid plan, and video calls for merchants on Pro and above. Chat is available only in the Built for Shopify app.

### What our support team **can** help you with

- Using the Quiz Builder
- Customizing the quiz design with our built-in tools
- Understanding the recommendation algorithm
- Integrating the quiz with other apps
- Interpreting quiz metrics and analytics
- Troubleshooting why a product is or is not recommended in the results

We answer every query within **2-3 hours on the same business day**. If your question needs more than that, we offer a live one-to-one call.

### What we **cannot** do for you

- Add custom behaviors with JavaScript (your developer should handle this)
- Add custom styles with CSS (your developer should handle this)
- Debug your WooCommerce store setup or configuration (your developer should handle this)
- Debug issues related to your Shopify theme (your developer or the theme’s developer should handle this)
- Create or edit Klaviyo templates (please contact a [Klaviyo template developer](https://www.fiverr.com/gigs/klaviyo-template) for this)

**Please note:** the app lets you add custom JavaScript and CSS to your quiz. We are a small team and cannot supply or maintain that code. Your developer has to handle it. Without an in-house developer, you can hire a freelancer on [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/) or [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/).

## Can you build a quiz for me?

**No.** Our support team explains how the app works and points you to the right articles and videos. We do not build quizzes.

To have a quiz built and styled for you, hire a freelancer on [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/) or [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/). Any front-end developer can handle quiz building and styling. Search those platforms for `quiz` to find someone who has done it before.
