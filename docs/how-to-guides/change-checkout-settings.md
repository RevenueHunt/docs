# How to Change Checkout Settings on Your Results Page

The checkout settings decide where the customer goes from the results page: the cart, the checkout, or the product page.

To change your checkout settings:

=== "Shopify"

    <div style="position: relative; padding-bottom: 56.34837355718783%; height: 0;"><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/GffASG1dDQo?si=2N3Hmmq_PMgqSaUx" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%;"></iframe></div>

    ![Results Page settings with the Checkout options section](/images/manual_shopifyV2_quizbuilder_quizbuilder_resultspage_resultspages_resultspagesettings.png)

    1. Open the [Results page](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/).
    2. Open the [Results page settings](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/#results-page-settings).
    3. Find the `Checkout options` section and open it.
    4. Select the `Bar options` you want to use.

        `Show bar` - Uncheck to hide the checkout bar.

        `Show "retake quiz" button` - Uncheck to hide the "retake quiz" button.

        `Show cart/checkout options` - Select the settings of the cart/checkout options.

        Cart/checkout options:

        `Enable "Add all to cart" button` - Uncheck to hide the "Add all to cart" button from the footer. When checked, the button appears only while none of the recommended products are in the cart.

        `Next destination` - Choose where the customer goes after the results page.

        `Proceed to checkout` - The customer goes straight to checkout after the products are added to the cart.

        `Proceed to cart` - The customer goes to the cart page after the products are added.

    5. Click the top-right `Save` button to update the preview and the live quiz.


=== "Shopify (Legacy)"

    <div style="position: relative; padding-bottom: 56.34837355718783%; height: 0;"><iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/f2ddc47043904e48bb08cbadc6f24233?sid=2f93b324-dc00-42ff-89cf-bd759e0f3a44" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%;"></iframe></div>

    1. **Access Results Page Settings**: Open the [Results Page](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/) tab in the [Quiz Builder](/reference/quiz-builder/). Click the cog icon to open the [Results Page settings](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/).

    2. **Locate Checkout Settings**: In the settings menu, open the `Basic` section and find `Checkout Settings`.
        ![Checkout Settings in the Basic section](/images/manual_quizbuilder_resultspage_settings_basic_checkout.png){width="500"}
    3. This is where you set how products are added to the cart and where the customer goes next. There are two options:

        1. **Add Product to Cart**:

            ![Add Product to Cart selected](/images/how_to_change_checkout_settings_add_to_cart.png)

            - Adds the selected products straight to the customer's cart, so they can go on to checkout.
            - After enabling this option, you can further specify whether customers should:
                -  `proceed to cart` and be directed to the shopping cart page to review their selections or if they should
                - `proceed to checkout` and be taken directly to the checkout page, bypassing the cart.

            Pick the one that suits your sales strategy.

        2. **Link to Product**:

            ![Link to Product selected](/images/how_to_change_checkout_settings_view_product.png)

            - Replaces the 'add to cart' action with a `view product` link, so the customer can read more about a product before buying.
            - The `view product` link opens the product page rather than adding the item to the cart. The customer sees the full description, the images and any reviews before they decide.

        3. **Publish the changes**: Click the top-right `Publish` button to update the preview/live quiz.

=== "Standalone"

    The Standalone version **cannot add products to the cart**. The customer is sent to the product page instead.

    ![Link to product is the only option in the Standalone version](/images/manual_standalone_quizbuilder_resultspage_checkoutsettings.png){width="500"}

    **Link to product** - Displays a "view product" button that takes the customer to the product page. This option disables the "add to cart" feature.

    !!! tip

        To add a product to the cart, build your own results page in your store and send the quiz data to it with the [Callback function](/how-to-guides/use-callback-function/).
