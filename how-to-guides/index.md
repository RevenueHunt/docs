# How-to Guides

Each guide covers one task, with a tab for every version of the app.

The seven groups below match the ones in the navigation. Each lists a few guides to start from.

## Getting started

Install the app, build a quiz, and move an old one across.

- **[Install the App](/how-to-guides/install-app/)** - Add the app to your store and open it for the first time.
- **[Create Your First Quiz](/how-to-guides/create-first-quiz/)** - Plan the questions, build the quiz, and publish it.
- **[Use Quiz Copilot](/how-to-guides/use-quiz-copilot/)** - The AI assistant inside the app, which answers questions and changes the quiz for you.
- **[Migrate a Legacy Quiz](/how-to-guides/migrate-shopify-legacy-quiz/)** - Move a quiz from the older Shopify app to the Built for Shopify one.

## Design and content

Change what the quiz looks like, and what it says.

- **[Customize the Quiz Design](/how-to-guides/customize-quiz-design/)** - Colors, fonts, layout, and your own CSS.
- **[Edit the Results Page](/how-to-guides/edit-results-page/)** - Build the last page from blocks, and make it react to the answers.
- **[Add a Welcome Screen](/how-to-guides/add-welcome-screen/)** - Open the quiz with a screen that sets expectations.
- **[Change the Language of Your Quiz](/how-to-guides/change-quiz-language/)** - Translate the wording, or run one quiz per language.
- **[A/B Test Your Quiz](/how-to-guides/ab-test-quiz/)** - Run two versions side by side and keep the one that performs better.

## Product recommendations

Decide which products the quiz suggests, and why.

- **[Recommend Products](/how-to-guides/recommend-products/)** - What a quiz can recommend, and which recommendation system to pick.
- **[Set Up a Scoring Quiz](/how-to-guides/set-up-scoring-quiz/)** - Score the answers against variables, then recommend by the winner.
- **[Set Up a Funnel Quiz](/how-to-guides/set-up-funnel-quiz/)** - Narrow the catalog question by question with upvotes.
- **[Filter Recommendations by Price](/how-to-guides/filter-recommendations-by-price/)** - Keep the results inside the range the customer picked.
- **[Troubleshoot Quiz Results](/how-to-guides/troubleshoot-product-results/)** - Find out why a product was recommended, or why it was not.

## Quiz logic and development

Send customers down different paths, and add your own code.

- **[Use Conditional Logic](/how-to-guides/use-conditional-logic/)** - Jump logic, skip logic and display logic, and what each one changes.
- **[Show or Hide Content Based on Quiz Answers](/how-to-guides/hide-content-with-logic/)** - One skincare quiz worked through all three logic types.
- **[Add a Discount to Your Quiz](/how-to-guides/add-discount/)** - Reward a finished quiz with a code.
- **[Add JavaScript to the Quiz](/how-to-guides/add-javascript/)** - Run your own code in the quiz and on the results page.
- **[Use Callback Function](/how-to-guides/use-callback-function/)** - Hand the quiz data to your own page when a customer finishes.

## Publishing and display

Put the quiz in front of customers.

- **[Publish a Quiz on Your Website](/how-to-guides/publish-quiz/)** - The six ways to place a quiz, on your store or as a link you share.
- **[Embed an Inline Quiz](/how-to-guides/publish-quiz-inline/)** - Put the quiz inside a page rather than in a popup.
- **[Set Up Automatic Popups](/how-to-guides/publish-quiz-automatic-popup/)** - Open the quiz on its own after a delay, or on exit intent.
- **[Redirect Quiz to Another Page](/how-to-guides/redirect-quiz-to-another-page/)** - Three ways to send a customer somewhere else at the end.
- **[Change Checkout Settings](/how-to-guides/change-checkout-settings/)** - Decide whether the results page adds to the cart or links to the product.

## Integrations and data

Send what the quiz collects to the tools you already use.

- **[Send Quiz Leads to Your CRM](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-crm/)** - Klaviyo, Mailchimp, HubSpot, Zapier and the rest.
- **[Set Up Result Emails](/how-to-guides/send-result-emails/)** - Email each customer their own results.
- **[Track Quiz Performance with Google Analytics](/how-to-guides/integrate-google-analytics/)** - See how customers move through the quiz, and where they leave it.
- **[Track Quiz Revenue](/how-to-guides/track-quiz-revenue/)** - Measure the sales your quizzes bring in.
- **[Download Quiz Responses](/how-to-guides/download-quiz-responses/)** - Export the answers as a file.

## Troubleshooting and maintenance

Fix a quiz that is not behaving.

- **[Import Your Catalog](/how-to-guides/sync-catalog/)** - Bring your products into the quiz, and refresh them later.
- **[why products are not added to the cart](/how-to-guides/add-to-cart-issues/)** - The usual causes, and what to check first.
- **[Check Quiz Loading Speed](/how-to-guides/check-quiz-loading-speed/)** - Measure what the quiz costs the page it sits on.
- **[Fix the Mobile Viewport Issue](/how-to-guides/fix-viewport-issue-on-mobile/)** - Stop the quiz zooming in when a customer taps a field.
- **[Copy the Quiz from One Store to Another](/how-to-guides/copy-the-quiz-from-one-store-to-another/)** - Move a finished quiz to a second store.
