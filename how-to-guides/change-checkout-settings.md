# How to Change Checkout Settings on Your Results Page

The checkout settings decide where a customer goes from the results page: to the cart, to the checkout, or to the product page.

## Open the checkout settings

=== "Shopify"

    <div style="position: relative; padding-bottom: 56.34837355718783%; height: 0;"><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/GffASG1dDQo?si=2N3Hmmq_PMgqSaUx" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%;"></iframe></div>

    1. **Open the [Results page](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/) tab in the Quiz builder.**

    2. **Open the [Results page settings](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/#results-page-settings).**

        ![Results Page settings with the Checkout options section](/images/manual_shopifyV2_quizbuilder_quizbuilder_resultspage_resultspages_resultspagesettings.png)

    3. **Open the `Checkout options` section.**

    The section holds the bar that sits at the foot of the results page.

    `Show bar` - Clear it to hide the checkout bar altogether.

    `Show "retake quiz" button` - Clear it to hide the retake button.

    `Show cart/checkout options` - Opens the cart and checkout settings, covered in [Add the products to the cart](#add-the-products-to-the-cart).

## Add the products to the cart

=== "Shopify"

    In `Checkout options`, turn on `Show cart/checkout options`.

    `Enable "Add all to cart" button` - Clear it to take the button out of the footer. While it is on, the button shows only when none of the recommended products are in the cart yet.

    `Next destination` - Where the customer goes once the products are in the cart.

    - `Proceed to cart` sends them to the cart page, where they can review what the quiz picked.
    - `Proceed to checkout` sends them straight to checkout, past the cart.

    Then click the top-right `Save` button.

=== "Shopify (Legacy)"

    Select `Add product to cart`. The results page then adds the recommended products to the cart, and the customer carries on to buy them.

    ![Add Product to Cart selected](/images/how_to_change_checkout_settings_add_to_cart.png)

    Choose where they land next:

    - `proceed to cart` sends them to the cart page, where they can review what the quiz picked.
    - `proceed to checkout` sends them straight to checkout, past the cart.

    Then click the top-right `Publish` button.

=== "WooCommerce"

    Select `Add product to cart`. The results page then adds the recommended products to the cart, and the customer carries on to buy them.

    ![Add Product to Cart selected](/images/how_to_change_checkout_settings_add_to_cart.png)

    Then set `Cart URL` to where they land next:

    - `/cart` sends them to the cart page, where they can review what the quiz picked.
    - `/checkout` sends them straight to checkout, past the cart.

    Then click the top-right `Publish` button.

=== "Magento"

    Select `Add product to cart`. The results page then adds the recommended products to the cart, and the customer carries on to buy them.

    ![Add Product to Cart selected](/images/how_to_change_checkout_settings_add_to_cart.png)

    The customer goes to the cart, where they can review what the quiz picked.

    Then click the top-right `Publish` button.

=== "BigCommerce"

    Select `Add product to cart`. The results page then adds the recommended products to the cart, and the customer carries on to buy them.

    ![Add Product to Cart selected](/images/how_to_change_checkout_settings_add_to_cart.png)

    The customer goes to the cart, where they can review what the quiz picked.

    Then click the top-right `Publish` button.

=== "Standalone"

    !!! note "Not available in this version"

        There is no cart to add to here. The results page links to the product page instead, as [Link to the product page](#link-to-the-product-page) describes.

        To build cart behavior of your own, send the quiz data to your own results page with a [callback function](/how-to-guides/use-callback-function/).

## Link to the product page

=== "Shopify"

    In this version the product link is a component of the product card, not a checkout setting. Swap the button on the card.

    1. **Open the [Results page](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/) tab and select your [Product block](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/#product-product-variants-collections).**

    2. **Open [`Product components layout`](/reference/quiz-builder/results-page/#slot-item-composition) in the block settings.**

    3. **Remove the `Add to cart button` component.**

    4. **Click `+ block` and add `Link to product`.** It adds a button that opens the product URL.

        ![manual_shopifyV2_quizbuilder_quizbuilder_resultspage_resultspages_blocksettings_productcomponents_linktoproduct](/images/manual_shopifyV2_quizbuilder_quizbuilder_resultspage_resultspages_blocksettings_productcomponents_linktoproduct.png)

    5. **Click the top-right `Save` button.**

    !!! warning "The link only opens on a live storefront"

        It does nothing in the quiz preview, or on a password-protected store.

=== "Shopify (Legacy)"

    Select `Link to product`. The results page then shows a `view product` button in place of the add to cart action, and the customer reads the product page before deciding.

    ![Link to Product selected](/images/how_to_change_checkout_settings_view_product.png)

    Choosing this turns the add to cart feature off.

    Then click the top-right `Publish` button.

=== "WooCommerce"

    Select `Link to product`. The results page then shows a `view product` button in place of the add to cart action, and the customer reads the product page before deciding.

    ![Link to Product selected](/images/how_to_change_checkout_settings_view_product.png)

    Choosing this turns the add to cart feature off.

    Then click the top-right `Publish` button.
