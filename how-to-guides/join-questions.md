# How to Display Multiple Questions per Slide in Your Quiz

A quiz that asks one thing per screen gets long. Putting several questions on one slide shortens it, and keeps the customer moving.

=== "Shopify"

    <div style="position: relative; padding-bottom: 56.34837355718783%; height: 0;"><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/C927w-0ZRl4?si=5j8Uwb7CLT-n-m7A" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%;"></iframe></div>

    In this version a question is a slide, and every block you add to it shows on that slide. There is nothing to join.

    1. **Open the [Quiz builder](/reference/quiz-builder/) and click `+ Add question`.** That adds the slide.

    2. **Click [`+ Add block`](/reference/quiz-builder/questions/#block-settings) and pick the [block](/reference/quiz-builder/questions/#question-types) for your first question.**

    3. **Click `+ Add block` again and pick the block for the second question.** Both now sit on the same slide.

        ![Two questions sharing one slide](https://loom.com/i/75de3b0357fc46919b8cf40d51a94975?workflows_screenshot=true)

    4. **Repeat for every question you want on the slide.** Two blocks of the same kind are fine, such as two text fields for a first and last name.

    5. **Click the top-right `Save` button, then take the quiz.** The questions should appear together, with one button at the end of them.

    !!! tip "Starting a new slide"

        Click `+ Add question` again. Blocks stay with the question they were added to.

=== "Shopify (Legacy)"

    <div style="position: relative; padding-bottom: 56.34837355718783%; height: 0;"><iframe src="https://www.loom.com/embed/651d6cb1573c4a53981b971cf9eefaa6?sid=b1ca7d15-e169-4acb-87b4-c994a0146c8b" frameborder="0" webkitallowfullscreen mozallowfullscreen allowfullscreen style="position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%;"></iframe></div>

    Here each question is a slide of its own. `Join next question` merges a question with the one below it, and you chain the setting down the list.

    1. **Add the questions you want to share a slide, one after another.** They join in the order they sit in the builder.

    2. **Click the first of them, then click the `wrench` icon to open its [question settings](/reference/quiz-builder/questions/#question-settings).**

    3. **Turn on `Join next question`.**

        ![The Join next question toggle in the question settings](/images/how_to_join_slides_setting.png)

    4. **Click the next question down and turn on `Join next question` there too.** Repeat down the list, but leave it off for the last question, which has nothing below it to join.

    5. **Check the builder.** A dashed line runs between joined questions where a slide break would otherwise be.

        ![Four questions joined into one slide](/images/how_to_join_slides_slides_joined.png)

    6. **Click the top-right `Publish` button, then take the quiz.** The questions should appear together, with one button at the end of them.
