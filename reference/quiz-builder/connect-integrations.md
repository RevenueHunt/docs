# Connect / Integrations

=== "Shopify"

    In the Integrations tab of the Quiz builder, you can connect your quiz to third-party services.

    !!! info "Currently integrated with"

        - [Shopify Customers (Shopify Flow)](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-shopify-customers/)
        - [Klaviyo](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-klaviyo/)
        - [Omnisend](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-omnisend/)
        - [HubSpot](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-hubspot/)
        - [Mailchimp](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-mailchimp/)
        - [Google Analytics](/how-to-guides/integrate-google-analytics/)
        - [Meta Pixel](/how-to-guides/integrate-meta-pixel/)
        - [Webhooks](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-webhooks/)
        - [Zapier](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-zapier/)

    To open the Integrations tab, go to the [Dashboard](/reference/dashboard/) and open the [Quiz settings](/reference/quiz-builder/quiz-settings/) page.

    ![Opening Quiz settings from the dashboard](/images/manual_shopifyv2_quizbuilder_integrations_opensettings.png)

    Then navigate to the `Integrations` tab.

    ![The Integrations tab, with the filter tabs and the service cards](/images/manual_shopifyV2_quizbuilder_integrations.png)

    A banner sits above the cards.

    `Get help` - Opens a chat about connecting your quiz.

    `Missing an app` - Tells the team which integration you would like added.

    Each service has its own card. Find the service, then follow the instructions on the card or under `Read more`. The button on a card depends on the service:

    `Activate` - Switches on a service that needs no credentials, such as [Google Analytics](/how-to-guides/integrate-google-analytics/) or [Meta Pixel](/how-to-guides/integrate-meta-pixel/).

    `Connect` - Links a service you have pasted a key into, such as [Zapier](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-zapier/).

    `+ Add webhook` - Adds a [webhook](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-webhooks/) endpoint.

    `Disconnect` - Appears once a service is connected. Click it to disconnect.

    Use the filter tabs above the cards to narrow the list:

    `All` - Every integration, the view the tab opens on.

    `Automations` - Connect to services like [Zapier](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-zapier/) or [Webhooks](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-webhooks/) and redirect your quiz data anywhere.

    ![The Automations filter, showing Webhooks and Zapier](/images/manual_shopifyV2_quizbuilder_integrations_automations.png)

    `Analytics & tracking` - Integrate with platforms like [Google Analytics](/how-to-guides/integrate-google-analytics/) and [Meta Pixel](/how-to-guides/integrate-meta-pixel/) to track quiz events, measure conversions, and understand customer behavior.

    ![The Analytics and tracking filter, showing Google Analytics and Meta Pixel](/images/manual_shopifyV2_quizbuilder_integrations_analytics.png)

    !!! info
        [Google Analytics](/how-to-guides/integrate-google-analytics/) and [Meta Pixel](/how-to-guides/integrate-meta-pixel/) connect directly to the property set up in your store. If you have not set up tracking for your store yet, you should do it before connecting the quiz to these services.

    `Mailing & CRMs` - Sync captured quiz leads directly to your email marketing or CRM platforms, such as [Klaviyo](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-klaviyo/), [Omnisend](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-omnisend/) or [HubSpot](/how-to-guides/send-leads-to-hubspot/). Use this data for personalized campaigns, segmentation, and automated follow-ups.

    ![The Mailing and CRMs filter, showing Klaviyo, Omnisend and HubSpot](/images/manual_shopifyV2_quizbuilder_integrations_mailing.png)
